📖 Game ke rules
  • 52 cards sab players mein barabar baant diye jate hain.
  • Jis ke pas Ace of Spades ♠ hai wohi game shuru karta hai (pehla card yahi chalna hai).
  • Har round mein pehle card ki suit follow karni hai. Suit na ho to koi bhi card chal do = Thulla.
  • Thulla hote hi round ruk jata hai. Jis ne us suit ka sab se bara card chala wo saray cards utha leta hai aur agla round wohi shuru karta hai.
  • Agar sab ne suit follow ki to cards discard ho jate hain, sab se bare card wala agla round shuru karta hai.
  • Jis ke cards pehle khatam ho wo bach gaya. Aakhir mein jis ke pas cards reh jayen wo Bhabhi 🫏
